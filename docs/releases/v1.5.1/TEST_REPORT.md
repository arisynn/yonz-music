## Limits

No independent physical Android16/HyperOS Gallery reindex test, no guarantee old Gallery tiles disappear immediately, no new Bluetooth/headset or long-duration/background matrix, no new live YouTube/other-platform transfer validation and no independent Play Protect classification. The1.5.0 owner-reported streaming/download feedback and network restrictions remain historical evidence, not new results. .nomedia may also hide the chosen folder from third-party MediaStore music players; Yono's explicit SAF library still reads it. All contents and subfolders of the selected download destination are affected. External/user markers and .cover markers are preserved when the option is off. No automatic legacy JSON relocation, mass .weba rename, conversion, media-database deletion, broad-storage permission or music-file cleanup is used.

Exact build commit, final signed APK sizes/hashes, package/native inspection and tested-runtime equivalence are appended to release artifacts by packaging. Prior detailed verification is preserved at docs/releases/v1.5.0 and docs/verification/v1.5.0-qa.
